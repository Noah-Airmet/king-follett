Manuscript Verification Notes, VIII. Analysis and Commentary (subsections A–H), IX. Sources and Bibliography — are the only place that commentary lives. Create `content/` with these Markdown files, each with a small YAML front matter (title, slug, updated: today's date from `date '+%Y-%m-%d'`):

1. `content/introduction.md` — what the edition is, the five texts and their sigla with T clearly explained as derived, the base-text choice and its limits, how the sections were made, what the apparatus records (quote the criteria from `data/apparatus.json` metadata rather than restating them differently). Numbers (word counts, variant counts, section counts) must NOT be hardcoded — write them as template placeholders of the form `{{stats.total_variants}}`, `{{stats.words.B}}`, `{{stats.sections}}` and list every placeholder you used at the end of the file in an HTML comment, so the site builder can fill them from data/.

2. `content/witnesses.md` — one subsection per witness (B W R C, then T), drawing the document/role/citation facts from `data/witnesses.json` (state them; do not embellish), plus 2–4 sentences on each report's character as a record, hedged and sourced (Smith pages where he characterizes the reports; JSP historical introductions as reported in `refs/jsp-markup-audit.md`).

3. `content/reading-the-apparatus.md` — how to read an entry (lemma ] readings; om.; the ‖ T divider; the types; the "canc." notation for cancellations; what "derived" means), with one worked example taken from the current `data/apparatus.json`.

4. `content/textual-history.md` — NEW: a compact account (600–900 words) of how the received text was built — the four reports → Bullock and Clayton's fusion → *Times and Seasons* → later *History of the Church* revisions → modern amalgamations (Larson 1978; Ehat and Cook 1980) — every factual claim with a page cite to Smith or a JSP source. This replaces the old site's unstated assumption that the four notes are the whole story.

5. `content/verification.md` — REPLACES old Section VI. The old text claimed "All 15 high-priority variants were verified against JPG scans" by an earlier AI model; those scans are gone and those verdicts are unrepeatable. The new statement must say plainly: the edition accepts the Joseph Smith Papers' transcriptions as authoritative; where JSP itself flags an uncertain reading (its "TEXT: Possibly ..." footnotes, listed in `data/footnotes.json` with kind "textual") the edition records it in the apparatus; the January 2026 scan notes are preserved in the data as `legacy_verification` for transparency but are not relied on; and the contested readings that most affect interpretation (immortal/coequal; Clayton's "from the time he had"; Richards's "Lot/lot"; Richards's "intelligence of a god") are listed with what the evidence actually is (which witnesses read what; what T did with it; what Smith says). No claim of verification the project cannot back.

6. `content/commentary.md` — the old Section VIII (A–H), revised: keep the substance and structure, but (a) re-check every variant reference (V-numbers, readings, witness attributions) against the revised `data/apparatus.json` and fix or remove anything that no longer holds; (b) hedge: replace assertions of what Joseph Smith "taught" or what a variant "proves" with what the reports say and what could be inferred; mark speculation as speculation (the "Richards caught both words" hypothesis is a hypothesis); drop superlatives like "theologically explosive" and "single most important" unless attributed to a source; (c) add T's readings where they change the story (e.g., what the published text did with immortal/coequal); (d) cite Smith where he discusses the same variant. Keep each subsection roughly its current length; do not pad.
